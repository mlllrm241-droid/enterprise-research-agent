# AI Supplier Assessment Standard

## Security

AI technology suppliers should provide clear information
about data storage, data isolation, access control,
security incident response and model usage policies.

## Compliance

Suppliers should identify applicable privacy,
security and regulatory obligations.

## Business Continuity

Critical suppliers should provide information about
service availability, disaster recovery and major
service dependency risks.

## Financial Risk

The assessment should consider revenue stability,
customer concentration, major capital requirements
and factors that may affect long-term service continuity.

## Technology Dependency

The assessment should examine dependence on
critical hardware, cloud platforms, third-party
software and external supply chains.
